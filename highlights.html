GREEN-X booth at the Digital Transformation Expo

GREEN-XKhánh Hòa

At Khanh Hoa Digital Transformation Day 2026, GREEN-X brought four groups of solutions: green cities, sustainable tourism, mariculture and marine conservation.

The busiest stop was the ocean robot GREEN-X built with VinaTech. Seeing a machine made for the water up close brought marine technology much closer to visitors.

Behind the booth was the part I looked after: re-checking the lists sent to the organisers, checking the booth design, arranging the layout, and working with the booth contractor and the organisers so everything was ready before opening.

Back in Khanh Hoa: green tourism, Hon Mun and mariculture

Field tripIoT

Shortly after Can Gio, the GREEN-X team returned to Khanh Hoa for two stories: green tourism and mariculture.

In Nha Trang, the team met the provincial Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism about the Green Tourism standard, and prepared to deploy the IoT biodiversity-monitoring system at Hon Mun.

At Van Phong, the team visited Australis’ high-tech sea-bass farm to discuss how AI can enter mariculture.

06.2026Can Gio, Ho Chi Minh City

Surveying the Can Gio mangroves

Field tripFieldwork

The GREEN-X team met the Can Gio protection-forest board, then took a boat out for a field survey through the mangroves.

The system presented combines remote sensing, IoT, AI and a digital twin to monitor and conserve the forest. The shared aim is to make Can Gio a living lab for conservation technology.

09–12.2023Quy Nhon, Binh Dinh

Internship at FPT AI Creative Valley

FPT AI ValleyQuy Nhon

In September 2023, I went to Quy Nhon with 52 FPT University students for a three-month internship at FPT AI Creative Valley (AI Valley). It was where what we had learned in class met the rhythm of a real development team.

As an AI Intern, I worked on three projects. The first analysed Vietnamese user reviews with PhoBERT and XLM-R. The other two were a Smart Menu application and a Vietnamese handwriting dataset for an OCR competition.
