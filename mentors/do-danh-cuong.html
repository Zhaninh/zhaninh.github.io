About

Assistant Professor at the College of Engineering and Computer Science (CECS), VinUniversity, where he leads the MESSI Lab (Multidisciplinary Exploration for Sustainable Systems and Intelligence). He was previously a research associate in the Engineering Department, University of Cambridge (2013–2017).

Research areas

MEMS sensors and low-power circuits; energy harvesting and wireless sensor networks; smart-grid and EV-charging optimisation; AI for biomedical signals (ECG) and point-of-care diagnostics.

Education

  • BS in Electronics, Vietnam National University, Hanoi (2004)
  • MEng in Electronics and Semiconductor, Chungbuk National University, Korea (2007)
  • PhD in Electronics Engineering, Cork Institute of Technology, Ireland (2012)
