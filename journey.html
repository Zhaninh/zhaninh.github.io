Research journey

Stages of study and research since 2021, and the plan ahead.

scroll down to continue ↓
  1. 2021–2025

    FPT University

    Bachelor in Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence specialisation. Capstone project: visual speech recognition for Vietnamese.

    The main difficulty of the capstone was data rather than models: Vietnamese had almost no public lip-reading dataset, so the team had to build one from scratch.

    Vietnamese lip reading

  2. 2025–2026

    VinBigData

    An intensive programme in computer vision, natural language processing, generative AI and MLOps, including a project on deep-learning detection of intracranial aneurysms.

    Intracranial aneurysm detection

  3. 2026–

    GREEN-X, VinUniversity

    Research Assistant, responsible for building NTCoral, a Vietnamese coral reef image dataset, together with marine biologists.

    Field conditions are harsher than the lab: equipment deployed at sea does not always run reliably, and each failure sends the team back to the design.

    NTCoral

  4. Next stage

    The route is clear: a master’s degree, a PhD and a postdoc abroad, then a return to Vietnam to teach at a university. What is not yet known is where. The intended specialisation is edge AI for field monitoring. This stretch is long.
