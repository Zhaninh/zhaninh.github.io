Intracranial aneurysm project

Small intracranial aneurysms on CTA, MRA or MRI are easy to miss, even for experienced radiologists. The project proposes a two-stage deep-learning pipeline on the RSNA Intracranial Aneurysm dataset: a 3D U-Net segments candidate vascular regions with high sensitivity, then 3D patches around each candidate are classified for aneurysm presence and anatomical site.

The dataset combines CTA, MRA and MRI from many sites, with aneurysm locations annotated by expert radiologists across 13 anatomical sites (Figure 1); this variety of modalities is the main challenge for the model.

The research was led by Pham Thi Thao Nguyen; my part was the writing, bringing the clinical context, the pipeline and the team’s results together into a chapter of Intelligent Precision Healthcare (CRC Press).
