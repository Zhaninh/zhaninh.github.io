NTCoral

N. N. G. Ninh, et al.

GREEN-X, VinUniversity

On the journey →

An expert-verified image dataset of Vietnamese coral reefs, built from underwater video surveys.

Why it matters

Public coral datasets come mostly from other regions. Vietnamese reefs have very few labelled images, so nobody yet knows how far existing models transfer or how many local labels are needed.

What I do

  • Lead the pipeline from survey video to released images: video review, frame selection, quality checks, annotation hand-off.
  • Coordinate genus-level annotation with marine biologists.
  • Design frame selection: remove near-duplicates, screen quality, keep coverage across survey sites.
  • Evaluate few-shot segmentation models on local data.

Pipeline

  1. Survey video
  2. Frame selection
  3. Quality loop
  4. Model proposals
  5. Expert verification
  6. Open dataset

My role

Data lead; first author of the paper in preparation

Citation

N. N. G. Ninh, et al. NTCoral: an expert-annotated image dataset of Vietnamese coral reefs (working title). Dataset paper in preparation, first author.
