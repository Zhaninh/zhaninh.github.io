Team project, not published · 2025

Vietnamese medicinal plant classification

FPT University · with Dam Nguyen Quynh Hoa and Pham Thi Thao Nguyen

Benchmarking deep learning models on 255 Vietnamese medicinal plant species.

Context

VNPlant-200 (Nguyen Quoc Trung and Truong Hoang Vinh, 2021) was the first public image set of 200 Vietnamese medicinal plant species. Our team extended it to VNPlant-255: 55 more species, about 34,800 field photographs in total, with natural variation in light, background and viewpoint.

The goal was a benchmark for fine-grained classification, comparing CNNs, Transformers, hybrid and lightweight models under one setup.

Evaluation setup

  • 80/10/10 train/validation/test split, stratified by species.
  • Same 224 px resolution, batch size and 20 epochs for every model.
  • Accuracy, parameter count and compute cost recorded to judge deployability.

What I did

  • Trained and evaluated seven model families: ConvNeXtV2-Tiny, MaxViT-Tiny, CoAtNet-0, SwinV2-Tiny, Xception, Xception with MIGT, and YOLOv8 classification.
  • Managed the team’s data, weights and training logs on Kaggle.

Note

The team decided not to publish the paper. Images of the original 200 species belong to VNPlant-200 and are not redistributed here.
